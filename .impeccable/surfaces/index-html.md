# Home surface

## Scope and mode

`app.html` and the prerendered React application it mounts. Visitor mode: Experience, with recruiter-speed orientation and editorial depth.

## Audience, job, action

Recruiters should identify Rushil's current role and focus in seconds. Engineers should be able to inspect a small set of real projects and writing. Design-aware visitors should feel an authored system without the interface becoming the subject. The primary actions are exploring selected work, reading an article, and following a profile link.

Rushil is based in Seattle, Washington. Location is factual context, not decorative header metadata.

## Proof and constraints

Use the user-provided LinkedIn profile export dated September 23, 2026 for current role, location, aperture and Sony history, and USC research; use the tracked site for historical projects and four real article links. Keep confidential work high-level. Do not ship unverified metrics, fake case studies, fake imagery, or the in-progress mission-control framing. Preserve accessible semantics, reduced-motion behavior, ordinary scrolling, and GitHub Pages compatibility.

## Chosen direction

Registration Boundary: graphite and bone planes are divided by a movable vermilion edge. The name occupies two typographic layers and remains the subject of the opening. The same boundary governs the current-work scene and changes the visibility of selected-work imagery. Geologica carries structural display language, Instrument Serif adds a controlled human counterpoint, and Martian Mono is reserved for exact metadata. On touch and keyboard, a visible range control moves the boundary. The selected concept is documented in `.impeccable/rebuild-forensics.md` and its executable prototype captures live in `.impeccable/prototypes/`.
